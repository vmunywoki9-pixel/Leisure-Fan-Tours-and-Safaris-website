Safari Overview

7 Days Kenya Wildlife Safari

Discover Kenya's spectacular wildlife and breathtaking landscapes on this seven-day safari adventure through three of the country's most iconic destinations.

Explore the legendary Maasai Mara, famous for its abundant wildlife and Big Five sightings. Continue to Lake Nakuru, renowned for its rhinos and beautiful scenery, before travelling to Amboseli for incredible elephant encounters with Mount Kilimanjaro as a backdrop.

The safari also includes a cultural experience in the Maasai Mara, allowing you to connect with local communities and learn about Maasai traditions and way of life.

This carefully designed itinerary provides two nights in each destination, allowing enough time for multiple game drives and a relaxed wildlife experience.

Cultural Experience

Connect With Maasai Culture

During your Maasai Mara stay, enjoy an opportunity to visit a local Maasai community and experience authentic aspects of traditional Maasai culture.

Meet local community members, experience a traditional welcome ceremony, enjoy songs and dances and learn about Maasai traditions and daily life.

You will also have an opportunity to learn about traditional beadwork and handicrafts and support local artisans by purchasing locally made crafts.
