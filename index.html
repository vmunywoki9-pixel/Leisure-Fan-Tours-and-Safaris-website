About Us

Enjoy Luxury and Adventure Across Kenya

Leisure Fan Tours and Safaris is a trusted Kenyan tour company specializing in unforgettable wildlife safaris, beach holidays, mountain adventures and tailor-made travel experiences across East Africa.

Our goal is to create memorable journeys that combine authentic Kenyan experiences, comfortable accommodation, professional service and carefully planned itineraries.

From the dramatic landscapes of the Maasai Mara to the beautiful beaches of the Kenyan Coast, we help travellers explore Kenya with comfort, confidence and unforgettable experiences.
