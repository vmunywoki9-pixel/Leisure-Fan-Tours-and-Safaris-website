DISCOVER EAST AFRICA

From Amboseli to Serengeti

Experience an unforgettable cross-border safari from the spectacular landscapes of Amboseli National Park in Kenya to the legendary endless plains of Serengeti National Park in Tanzania. Enjoy breathtaking views of Mount Kilimanjaro, encounter some of Africa's most iconic wildlife and experience incredible game drives across two world-renowned safari destinations. This carefully designed flying safari is ideal for travelers who want to experience the best of Kenya and Tanzania within a short period.

Duration

4 Days / 3 Nights

Package Price

From USD 4,250 per person

Destinations

Amboseli National Park
Serengeti National Park

Safari Highlights

  • Spectacular views of Mount Kilimanjaro.
  • Experience Amboseli's famous elephant herds.
  • Enjoy morning and afternoon game drives.
  • Search for lions, cheetahs, leopards, buffaloes and other wildlife.
  • Fly from Amboseli to Tanzania to maximize your safari time.
  • Explore the legendary Serengeti National Park.
  • Search for the Big Five.
  • Experience the endless plains of the Serengeti.
  • Enjoy spectacular African sunsets.
  • Experience incredible wildlife photography opportunities.
  • Depending on season and location, search for Great Migration herds.

Detailed 4-Day Itinerary

Day 1: Nairobi → Amboseli National Park

🌅 Morning

  • Pick up from your hotel or airport in Nairobi.
  • Transfer to Wilson Airport.
  • Take a scheduled or charter flight to Amboseli.
  • Meet your safari guide and transfer to your lodge or luxury tented camp.

☀️ Afternoon

  • Lunch and check-in.
  • Relax and enjoy views of Mount Kilimanjaro.
  • Afternoon game drive in Amboseli National Park.
  • Search for large elephant herds, zebras, wildebeest, giraffes and buffaloes.

🌙 Evening

  • Return to your lodge or camp.
  • Enjoy an African sunset.
  • Dinner and overnight stay.

Overnight: Amboseli luxury lodge or camp

Meals: Lunch & Dinner

Day 2: Full-Day Amboseli National Park Safari

🌅 Morning

  • Early breakfast.
  • Morning game drive with Mount Kilimanjaro as your backdrop.
  • Explore Amboseli's wetlands and open plains.
  • Look for elephants, lions, cheetahs, buffaloes, hippos and bird species.
  • Enjoy wildlife photography.

☀️ Afternoon

  • Return to the lodge for lunch.
  • Relax and enjoy the lodge facilities.
  • Afternoon game drive.
  • Explore different areas of the park in search of wildlife.

🌙 Evening

  • Return to camp.
  • Enjoy sunset views.
  • Optional Maasai cultural experience, subject to availability.
  • Dinner and overnight stay.

Overnight: Amboseli luxury lodge or camp

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch & Dinner

Day 3: Amboseli → Serengeti National Park

🌅 Morning

  • Early breakfast.
  • Transfer to the airstrip.
  • Fly from Amboseli toward Tanzania, connecting to the Serengeti.
  • Arrive at the Serengeti airstrip.
  • Meet your Tanzanian safari guide.

☀️ Afternoon

  • Enjoy lunch at your lodge or camp.
  • Begin your first Serengeti game drive.
  • Explore the endless plains in search of lions, cheetahs, leopards, elephants, giraffes and buffaloes.
  • Depending on the season and migration location, search for wildebeest and zebra herds.

🌙 Evening

  • Return to your lodge or luxury tented camp.
  • Enjoy an unforgettable Serengeti sunset.
  • Dinner and overnight stay.

Overnight: Serengeti luxury lodge or camp

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch & Dinner

Day 4: Serengeti → Nairobi

🌅 Morning

  • Early breakfast.
  • Morning game drive in the Serengeti.
  • Explore the plains during the cooler morning hours when wildlife is most active.
  • Continue searching for the Big Five.

☀️ Afternoon

  • Return to the lodge or airstrip.
  • Lunch depending on flight schedule.
  • Fly from Serengeti toward Nairobi.

🌙 Evening

  • Arrive in Nairobi.
  • Transfer to your hotel or Jomo Kenyatta International Airport for your onward flight.

Meals: Breakfast & Lunch
