DISCOVER NORTHERN KENYA

The Northern Kenya Frontier Safari

Discover the remote and extraordinary landscapes of northern Kenya on a 10-day journey from Nairobi's wildlife conservation attractions to Samburu, Marsabit, the spectacular Chalbi Desert and the shores of Lake Turkana. This carefully designed adventure combines wildlife, conservation, culture, desert exploration, photography and authentic experiences in some of Kenya's least-visited destinations.

Duration

10 Days / 9 Nights

Package Price

USD 11,386 per person

Route

Nairobi – Samburu – Marsabit – Chalbi Desert – Lake Turkana

Safari Highlights

  • Visit the Giraffe Centre in Nairobi.
  • Visit the David Sheldrick Wildlife Trust and learn about elephant rescue and conservation.
  • Experience a wildlife safari in Samburu National Reserve.
  • Search for the Samburu Special Five: Grevy's zebra, reticulated giraffe, gerenuk, Beisa oryx and Somali ostrich.
  • Explore the unique volcanic ecosystem of Marsabit National Park.
  • Discover the vast and dramatic landscapes of the Chalbi Desert.
  • Enjoy a camel experience and 4x4 desert exploration.
  • Experience unforgettable desert sunsets and stargazing.
  • Explore the landscapes surrounding Lake Turkana, the Jade Sea.
  • Experience Samburu and Turkana culture and traditions.

Detailed 10-Day Itinerary

Day 1: Arrival in Nairobi – Giraffe Centre & Elephant Conservation

🌅 Morning

  • Arrive at Jomo Kenyatta International Airport.
  • Meet and greet with your Leisure Fan Tours and Safaris representative.
  • Private transfer to Serena Hotel.
  • Freshen up and have breakfast or brunch, depending on arrival time.

☀️ Afternoon

  • Visit the Giraffe Centre.
  • See and interact closely with the endangered Rothschild's giraffe.
  • Learn about giraffe conservation and environmental education.

🌙 Evening

  • Visit the David Sheldrick Wildlife Trust, subject to official visiting hours and booking requirements.
  • Learn about elephant rescue, rehabilitation and conservation.
  • Return to the hotel for a welcome dinner and safari briefing.

Day 2: Nairobi → Samburu

🌅 Morning

  • Breakfast at the hotel.
  • Depart Nairobi by private safari vehicle.
  • Drive north toward the Samburu region and enjoy scenic views of Kenya's changing landscapes.

☀️ Afternoon

  • Arrive in Samburu.
  • Check in at Elephant Bedroom Camp.
  • Enjoy lunch and time to relax while taking in the surrounding landscape.

🌙 Evening

  • Optional short nature walk or lodge experience, where available.
  • Enjoy a spectacular Samburu sunset.
  • Dinner and safari briefing for the following day.

Day 3: Samburu National Reserve Safari & Cultural Experience

🌅 Morning

  • Early breakfast.
  • Morning game drive in Samburu National Reserve.
  • Search for the Samburu Special Five.
  • Look for elephants, buffaloes, leopards and other wildlife.

☀️ Afternoon

  • Return to the camp for lunch.
  • Relax and enjoy the camp facilities.
  • Afternoon game drive and wildlife photography.

🌙 Evening

  • Visit a Samburu community.
  • Learn about traditional lifestyles, music and dances.
  • Return to the lodge for dinner and relax around the campfire.

Day 4: Samburu → Marsabit

🌅 Morning

  • Breakfast at the lodge and check out.
  • Depart Samburu and continue north toward Marsabit.
  • Enjoy scenic views of northern Kenya.

☀️ Afternoon

  • Arrive in Marsabit.
  • Check in at Marsabit Lodge and have lunch.
  • Relax at the lodge and explore the surrounding area.

🌙 Evening

  • Enjoy a guided nature experience or sunset viewing, where available.
  • Dinner at the lodge.
  • Relax and prepare for the following day's adventure.

Day 5: Marsabit National Park

🌅 Morning

  • Early breakfast.
  • Morning game drive in Marsabit National Park.
  • Explore the unique forested volcanic ecosystem.
  • Search for elephants, buffaloes, giraffes and bird species.
  • Visit the scenic Lake Paradise area.

☀️ Afternoon

  • Return for lunch.
  • Relax at the lodge.
  • Afternoon exploration or guided nature walk, where available.

🌙 Evening

  • Enjoy a sunset experience.
  • Optional cultural interaction with local communities.
  • Dinner and stargazing around the lodge.

Day 6: Marsabit → Chalbi Desert

🌅 Morning

  • Breakfast and check out.
  • Depart Marsabit for the spectacular Chalbi Desert.
  • Travel through remote northern Kenya landscapes.
  • Stop at scenic viewpoints for photography.

☀️ Afternoon

  • Enter the Chalbi Desert.
  • Explore the desert's dramatic landscapes.
  • Enjoy a 4x4 desert adventure.
  • Arrive at your desert camp for lunch and relaxation.

🌙 Evening

  • Enjoy a spectacular Chalbi Desert sunset.
  • Traditional bush dinner.
  • Experience the peaceful desert atmosphere.
  • Stargazing under the clear northern Kenya sky.

Day 7: Full-Day Chalbi Desert Adventure

🌅 Morning

  • Early morning desert sunrise.
  • Breakfast at camp.
  • Enjoy a camel experience.
  • Explore the desert landscape and take photographs.

☀️ Afternoon

  • Return to camp for lunch.
  • Relax during the hottest part of the day.
  • Afternoon 4x4 desert exploration.
  • Visit local communities, where available and appropriate.

🌙 Evening

  • Watch the sunset over the Chalbi Desert.
  • Enjoy a bush dinner.
  • Traditional storytelling and cultural entertainment.
  • Stargazing.

Day 8: Chalbi Desert → Lake Turkana

🌅 Morning

  • Breakfast at the desert camp.
  • Depart for Lake Turkana.
  • Travel through the remote northern frontier.
  • Enjoy scenic photography stops.

☀️ Afternoon

  • Arrive at Lake Turkana.
  • Check in at Lobolo Camp.
  • Lunch and relaxation.
  • Enjoy panoramic views of the Jade Sea.

🌙 Evening

  • Experience a spectacular Lake Turkana sunset.
  • Optional cultural interaction with the Turkana community.
  • Dinner by the lake.
  • Relax under the stars.

Day 9: Full-Day Lake Turkana Experience

🌅 Morning

  • Breakfast.
  • Explore the Lake Turkana region.
  • Optional boat excursion, subject to local conditions and availability.
  • Photography of the lake and surrounding volcanic landscapes.

☀️ Afternoon

  • Lunch.
  • Visit a local Turkana community.
  • Learn about traditional culture, crafts and lifestyle.
  • Explore the surrounding landscape.

🌙 Evening

  • Return to your camp.
  • Enjoy a final sunset overlooking Lake Turkana.
  • Farewell dinner.
  • Stargazing and relaxation.

Day 10: Lake Turkana → Nairobi

🌅 Morning

  • Breakfast at the camp.
  • Enjoy your final views of Lake Turkana.
  • Check out and prepare for departure.

☀️ Afternoon

  • Transfer to Lodwar Airport.
  • Take a scheduled flight toward Nairobi.

🌙 Evening

  • Arrive in Nairobi.
  • Transfer to Jomo Kenyatta International Airport for your onward flight.
