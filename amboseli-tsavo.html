Safari Overview

5 Days Amboseli & Tsavo Wildlife Safari

Experience an unforgettable five-day safari through three of Kenya's spectacular wildlife destinations: Amboseli National Park, Tsavo West National Park and Tsavo East National Park.

This safari offers opportunities to see large herds of elephants, lions, leopards, giraffes, zebras, buffaloes and Tsavo's famous red elephants.

The journey also includes spectacular views of Mount Kilimanjaro, a visit to Mzima Springs and extensive game drives across Kenya's beautiful savannah landscapes.

Package Details

Your Safari at a Glance

Duration

5 Days / 4 Nights

Safari Route

Nairobi → Amboseli → Tsavo West → Tsavo East → Nairobi

Transport

Private 4×4 Land Cruiser Safari Vehicle

Package Cost

Day 1

Nairobi → Amboseli National Park

Distance: Approximately 230–250 km | Driving time: Approximately 4–5 hours

🌅 Morning

•⁠ ⁠Pick-up from Jomo Kenyatta International Airport.

•⁠ ⁠Safari briefing with your professional driver-guide.

•⁠ ⁠Depart Nairobi and travel towards Amboseli.

•⁠ ⁠Enjoy scenic views of the Kenyan countryside.

☀️ Afternoon

•⁠ ⁠Arrive in Amboseli National Park.

•⁠ ⁠Check into Amboseli Sopa Lodge and enjoy lunch.

•⁠ ⁠Relax briefly.

•⁠ ⁠Afternoon game drive through Amboseli.

•⁠ ⁠Look for large elephant herds, lions, giraffes, zebras and buffaloes.

•⁠ ⁠Enjoy views of Mount Kilimanjaro, weather permitting.

🌙 Evening

•⁠ ⁠Return to your accommodation.

•⁠ ⁠Dinner and relaxation.

•⁠ ⁠Optional campfire experience.

Overnight: Amboseli

Meals: Lunch & Dinner

Day 2

Full-Day Amboseli National Park Safari

🌅 Morning

•⁠ ⁠Early morning sunrise game drive.

•⁠ ⁠Search for elephants and predators.

•⁠ ⁠Enjoy spectacular views of Mount Kilimanjaro.

•⁠ ⁠Return to the lodge for breakfast.

☀️ Afternoon

•⁠ ⁠Continue with a game drive through the park.

•⁠ ⁠Explore the Amboseli wetlands and open plains.

•⁠ ⁠Look for elephants, hippos, giraffes, zebras, wildebeest, buffaloes and lions.

•⁠ ⁠Enjoy lunch at the lodge.

🌙 Evening

•⁠ ⁠Return to your lodge.

•⁠ ⁠Dinner and relaxation.

•⁠ ⁠Optional Maasai cultural experience.

Overnight: Amboseli

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch & Dinner

Day 3

Amboseli → Tsavo West National Park

Distance: Approximately 150–200 km, depending on the route | Driving time: Approximately 3–4 hours

🌅 Morning

•⁠ ⁠Enjoy breakfast at your lodge.

•⁠ ⁠Check out and depart Amboseli.

•⁠ ⁠Travel towards Tsavo West National Park.

☀️ Afternoon

•⁠ ⁠Enter Tsavo West National Park.

•⁠ ⁠Check in and enjoy lunch at Kilaguni Serena Safari Lodge.

•⁠ ⁠Afternoon game drive.

•⁠ ⁠Explore the park in search of elephants, lions, buffaloes, giraffes and zebras.

•⁠ ⁠Visit Mzima Springs, where you may see hippos and crocodiles.

🌙 Evening

•⁠ ⁠Return to your lodge.

•⁠ ⁠Dinner and relaxation.

•⁠ ⁠Enjoy the atmosphere of the African wilderness.

Overnight: Tsavo West

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch & Dinner

Day 4

Tsavo West → Tsavo East National Park

Distance: Approximately 100–150 km, depending on the route and park gates.

Driving time: Approximately 3–4 hours, excluding game drives.

🌅 Morning

•⁠ ⁠Early morning game drive in Tsavo West.

•⁠ ⁠Search for wildlife and predators.

•⁠ ⁠Return for breakfast.

•⁠ ⁠Check out and begin your journey towards Tsavo East.

☀️ Afternoon

•⁠ ⁠Enter Tsavo East National Park.

•⁠ ⁠Check in and enjoy lunch at Ashnil Aruba Lodge.

•⁠ ⁠Afternoon game drive.

•⁠ ⁠Search for Tsavo's famous red elephants, lions, giraffes, zebras and buffaloes.

•⁠ ⁠Explore the park's expansive savannah landscapes.

🌙 Evening

•⁠ ⁠Return to the lodge.

•⁠ ⁠Dinner and relaxation.

•⁠ ⁠Optional campfire experience.

Overnight: Tsavo East

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch & Dinner

Day 5

Tsavo East → Nairobi

Distance: Approximately 300–350 km | Driving time: Approximately 5–7 hours

🌅 Morning

•⁠ ⁠Early morning final game drive in Tsavo East.

•⁠ ⁠Search for elephants, lions, leopards and other wildlife.

•⁠ ⁠Return to the lodge for breakfast.

☀️ Afternoon

•⁠ ⁠Check out and depart Tsavo East.

•⁠ ⁠Drive towards Nairobi.

•⁠ ⁠Stop for lunch along the way.

•⁠ ⁠Continue your journey to Nairobi.

🌙 Evening

•⁠ ⁠Arrive in Nairobi.

•⁠ ⁠Transfer to Jomo Kenyatta International Airport for your departure flight.

Meals: Breakfast & Lunch
