Itinerary

Day 1 – Nairobi to Amboseli

Private transfer from Nairobi to Amboseli National Park. Afternoon game drive followed by a luxury sundowner experience. Overnight at Tortilis Camp Amboseli.

Day 2 – Full-Day Amboseli Safari

Full-day wildlife safari with opportunities to see elephants, lions, cheetahs, giraffes, buffaloes and zebras, with spectacular views of Mount Kilimanjaro.

Day 3 – Amboseli to Tsavo West

Travel to Tsavo West National Park. Afternoon game drive and luxury stay at Severin Safari Camp.

Day 4 – Tsavo West to Tsavo East

Morning game drive followed by a transfer to Tsavo East. Afternoon game drive and overnight at Ashnil Aruba Lodge.

Day 5 – Tsavo East to Mombasa

Morning game drive followed by transfer to Mombasa. Check in at Sarova Whitesands Beach Resort & Spa.

Day 6 – Mombasa Old Town & Fort Jesus

Private guided cultural tour of Mombasa Old Town and Fort Jesus, followed by a Swahili cultural and culinary experience.

Day 7 – Mombasa to Diani Beach

Private transfer to Diani Beach. Check in at Baobab Beach Resort & Spa and enjoy a relaxing afternoon by the Indian Ocean.

Day 8 – Diani Beach Relaxation

Enjoy a free beach day with optional snorkelling, scuba diving, glass-bottom boat trips, kite surfing or other water activities.

Day 9 – Diani Beach Leisure & Culture

Leisure day with optional spa treatment, cultural experience, Kaya Kinondo Sacred Forest visit or additional beach activities.

Day 10 – Diani Beach to Nairobi / Departure

Final breakfast and beach relaxation followed by transfer to Ukunda Airport or private transfer to Nairobi for your departure flight.
