Safari Overview

5 Days Kenya Wildlife & Maasai Cultural Safari

Discover the best of Kenya's wildlife and culture on an unforgettable five-day journey through the Maasai Mara and Lake Nakuru.

This safari combines exciting game drives with an authentic Maasai cultural experience. Explore the Mara plains in search of the Big Five, enjoy a sunrise game drive and meet local Maasai communities.

Continue your adventure to Lake Nakuru National Park, famous for its black and white rhinos, Rothschild's giraffes and beautiful scenic landscapes.

The safari is ideal for travellers looking for a combination of wildlife, culture, photography and scenic experiences.

Day 1

Nairobi → Maasai Mara

Distance: Approximately 270–300 km | Driving time: Approximately 5–6 hours

🌅 Morning: Journey to the Maasai Mara

•⁠ ⁠Pick-up from Jomo Kenyatta International Airport by your tour driver.

•⁠ ⁠Tour briefing and introduction to your safari.

•⁠ ⁠Depart Nairobi early in the morning.

•⁠ ⁠Enjoy a scenic drive through the Great Rift Valley.

•⁠ ⁠Stop at a viewpoint for photographs and refreshments.

☀️ Afternoon: Arrival & First Game Drive

•⁠ ⁠Continue your journey to the Maasai Mara.

•⁠ ⁠Arrive and check in at AA Lodge.

•⁠ ⁠Enjoy lunch at the lodge.

•⁠ ⁠Relax briefly before your first afternoon game drive.

•⁠ ⁠Search for lions, elephants, giraffes, zebras, buffaloes, wildebeest and other wildlife.

🌙 Evening: Lodge Experience

•⁠ ⁠Return to your accommodation before sunset.

•⁠ ⁠Freshen up and enjoy dinner.

•⁠ ⁠Relax around the campfire.

•⁠ ⁠Listen to stories about Maasai traditions and life in the Mara.

Overnight: Maasai Mara

Accommodation: AA Lodge

Meals: Lunch & Dinner

Day 2

Full-Day Maasai Mara Wildlife & Cultural Experience

🌅 Morning: Sunrise Game Drive

•⁠ ⁠Wake up early for a sunrise game drive, one of the best times to see predators.

•⁠ ⁠Search for lions, leopards and other wildlife.

•⁠ ⁠Explore the Mara plains when wildlife is most active.

•⁠ ⁠Enjoy the beautiful sunrise over the savannah.

•⁠ ⁠Return to the lodge for breakfast.

☀️ Afternoon: Maasai Cultural Experience

•⁠ ⁠Visit a Maasai community.

•⁠ ⁠Experience a traditional Maasai welcome ceremony.

•⁠ ⁠Enjoy traditional songs and dances.

•⁠ ⁠Learn about Maasai traditions and way of life.

•⁠ ⁠Visit a traditional Maasai homestead (manyatta).

•⁠ ⁠Meet local Maasai people and interact with community members.

•⁠ ⁠Learn about traditional beadwork and handicrafts.

•⁠ ⁠Opportunity to purchase locally made crafts directly from artisans.

🌙 Evening: Maasai Cultural Dinner Experience

•⁠ ⁠Return to your lodge or camp.

•⁠ ⁠Enjoy dinner.

•⁠ ⁠Optional cultural storytelling around a campfire.

•⁠ ⁠Learn more about Maasai history, wildlife conservation and the relationship between communities and the Mara ecosystem.

Overnight: Maasai Mara

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch & Dinner

Day 3

Maasai Mara Full-Day Safari & Community Experience

Full-day wildlife exploration in the Maasai Mara.

🌅 Morning: Early Morning Game Drive

•⁠ ⁠Enjoy an early morning game drive in the Maasai Mara.

•⁠ ⁠Search for the Big Five.

•⁠ ⁠Focus on finding lions, leopards, cheetahs and other predators.

•⁠ ⁠Look for elephants, giraffes, zebras and wildebeest.

•⁠ ⁠Return to the lodge for breakfast.

☀️ Afternoon: Extended Game Drive & Picnic Lunch

•⁠ ⁠Enjoy lunch at the lodge.

•⁠ ⁠Continue exploring the Maasai Mara.

•⁠ ⁠Visit different wildlife habitats and viewpoints.

•⁠ ⁠Enjoy photography opportunities throughout the afternoon.

🌙 Evening: Relaxation & Local Interaction

•⁠ ⁠Return to the lodge or camp.

•⁠ ⁠Enjoy dinner.

•⁠ ⁠Relax around the campfire.

•⁠ ⁠Optional opportunity to interact with local Maasai guides or community representatives.

•⁠ ⁠Discuss wildlife conservation and the importance of tourism to local communities.

Overnight: Maasai Mara

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch & Dinner

Day 4

Maasai Mara → Lake Nakuru

Distance: Approximately 250–300 km

Driving time: Approximately 5–6 hours

🌅 Morning: Final Mara Game Drive

•⁠ ⁠Enjoy an early morning game drive.

•⁠ ⁠Search for wildlife you may have missed.

•⁠ ⁠Take final photographs of the Mara landscape.

•⁠ ⁠Return to the lodge for breakfast.

•⁠ ⁠Check out and depart for Lake Nakuru.

☀️ Afternoon: Arrival at Lake Nakuru

•⁠ ⁠Continue your journey towards Lake Nakuru.

•⁠ ⁠Arrive and enjoy lunch.

•⁠ ⁠Check in at Sarova Lion Hill Lodge.

•⁠ ⁠Take an afternoon game drive in Lake Nakuru National Park.

•⁠ ⁠Search for black and white rhinos, Rothschild's giraffes, buffaloes, zebras and lions.

🌙 Evening: Lodge Experience

•⁠ ⁠Return to your lodge.

•⁠ ⁠Enjoy dinner at the lodge.

•⁠ ⁠Relax and enjoy the evening.

•⁠ ⁠Optional discussion about Kenyan wildlife conservation and the importance of protecting rhinos.

Overnight: Lake Nakuru Area

Accommodation: Sarova Lion Hill Lodge

Meals: Breakfast, Lunch & Dinner

Day 5

Lake Nakuru → Nairobi

Distance: Approximately 160–180 km | Driving time: Approximately 3–4 hours

🌅 Morning: Final Wildlife Game Drive

•⁠ ⁠Enjoy an early morning game drive in Lake Nakuru National Park.

•⁠ ⁠Search for rhinos and other wildlife.

•⁠ ⁠Enjoy the beautiful scenery around the lake.

•⁠ ⁠Return to the lodge for breakfast.

☀️ Afternoon: Journey to Nairobi

•⁠ ⁠Check out and depart Lake Nakuru.

•⁠ ⁠Drive towards Nairobi.

•⁠ ⁠Stop along the way for lunch.

•⁠ ⁠Optional stop at a local craft market or souvenir shop.

🌙 Evening: Safari Ends

•⁠ ⁠Arrive in Nairobi.

•⁠ ⁠Transfer to Jomo Kenyatta International Airport for your international flight.

Meals: Breakfast & Lunch
